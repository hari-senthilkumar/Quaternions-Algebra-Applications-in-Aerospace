# Quaternions-Algebra-Applications-in-Aerospace

Abstract: This paper will examine the properties of quaternions (especially the quaternion rotation operator) and why they offer certain advantages compared to Euler angles when it comes to 3D modeling. We will begin by outlining the basic algebraic properties and theorems of quaternions before focusing specifically on quaternion rotation. From there, we will compare quaternion rotation to rotation done by Euler angles, primarily the aerospace and orbit ephemeris sequences.
